Rule the Hoop · basketball coaching game

Privacy policy

Last updated October 26, 2026

In short: Rule the Hoop doesn't collect, send or share any information about you. There are no accounts, ads, analytics or trackers. Your leagues are saved on your own device and nowhere else. Purchases are handled by Google Play or the App Store, not by the game.

What the app keeps, and where

Rule the Hoop saves the leagues you play (teams, rosters, schedules, results and the names you give your team or coach) so you can pick up where you left off. These saves stay on your device:

  • Android app: in the app's private storage on your phone. If you have Android's device backup turned on, Android may include them in your phone's backup to your Google account, as it does for most apps. That backup is handled by Google under your Google account settings; the game's developer can't see it.
  • iPhone app: in the app's private storage, which iOS may include in your iCloud or computer backups under your Apple account settings.
  • Browser version: in your browser's local storage for the game's page.

Deleting a league in the game deletes its save (and any game you left partway). Uninstalling the app deletes all of them.

Purchases

Each league has a free season; one-time purchases unlock the seasons after it. You buy them through Google Play (Android) or the App Store (iPhone), under your store account. The store handles the payment and your payment details; the game never sees them. The game asks the store which of its products your account owns (when you buy, when you tap Restore purchases, and on Android when the app starts) and remembers the answer on your device so it works offline. The developer receives the store's usual sales reports, which don't identify you to the game. Google's and Apple's own privacy policies cover what the stores do with your purchases.

What the app doesn't do

  • It doesn't ask for or collect your name, email address, location, contacts, photos or any other personal information.
  • It doesn't use advertising, analytics, crash reporting or tracking services, and it doesn't share or sell anything.
  • The game itself doesn't go online: the game, its fonts and its data are all inside the app. The only connection is to the store for purchases, as described above. (The browser version loads its typefaces from Google Fonts, which receives the usual request information a browser sends, such as your IP address. It has no purchases.)
  • It asks for no permissions beyond what every app has.

Children

Rule the Hoop collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If a future version of Rule the Hoop changes any of this, for example by adding an online feature, this page will be updated before that version is released, and the date above will change.
